# 01 — Vision, Mission & Goals

**Document type:** Product foundation  
**Version:** 2.0 (Vision 2.0 Local Companion)  
**Language:** English  
**Aligned with:** Work Plan `07-Decisions-Log.md` v2.0

---

## 1. Product name

**LocalLife AI**  
Tagline: *Your personal local companion*

---

## 2. One-sentence definition

LocalLife AI helps people live a place like a local — using Trusted Guide knowledge, who they are, and an AI that plans and advises (via a floating companion Avatar), not a generic map of the world.

---

## 3. Mission

Build the most trusted **local operating system** for everyday life in a city: transport, safety context, places, events, and personalized plans — grounded in verified zone knowledge.

---

## 4. Vision

Anyone — tourist, student, worker, visitor, family — feels oriented and safe making precise choices in an unfamiliar place.

---

## 5. Brand promise

> Don’t just visit a place. Understand it. Plan it. Live it like someone who belongs there.

---

## 6. Strategic positioning

LocalLife is **not** Google Maps and **not** a generic chatbot.

| Reference | What we take | What we improve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | Conversation | Grounding in Trusted Guide DB + hard profile filters + plans |
| Google Maps | Pins / GPS context | “Why / when / for whom / how locals do it” + offline plans |
| TripAdvisor | Reviews | Daily-life ops + Guide comments + audience fit — beyond tourism |

**Differentiator:** Guide-owned zone knowledge + Client identity + AI plans + Avatar companion.

---

## 7. Core value proposition

One companion that knows:

- the zone (Trusted Guide / SubGuide knowledge, freshness)
- who you are (purpose, conservatism, budget, mobility)
- what to do next (editable offline plan + explainability)

---

## 8. Product thesis

People do not only need **points on a map**.  
They need **operating instructions** and a **plan for their next hours**.

---

## 9. Success metrics (Vision 2.0)

- Plan created / saved from Chat or packs
- Avatar notification engagement (open → action)
- Guide content freshness (share updated in last 30 days)
- SubGuide proposals confirmed by Admin
- Grounded AI answers (citation rate)
- Report → verified update → replan completion

---

## 10. Non-goals (near term)

- Turn-by-turn navigation as the product
- Open global UGC map spam
- Booking/payments
- Social feed
