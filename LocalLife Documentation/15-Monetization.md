# 15 — Monetization

**Document type:** Business model  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Language:** English

---

## 1. Principle

Earn money **without destroying trust**.  
If users stop believing recommendations, the company dies.

---

## 2. Revenue streams

### 2.1 Sponsored places
Businesses promote restaurants, cafés, attractions, activities.  
Always labeled as sponsored in UI and AI answers.

### 2.2 Sponsored events
Organizers boost concerts, festivals, workshops to relevant audiences.

### 2.3 Booking commission (future)
Commission on experiences, activities, events, restaurant bookings.

### 2.4 Premium subscription (future)
- offline city guides
- advanced AI planning
- unlimited saved itineraries
- early access features

### 2.5 Business dashboard (future)
Analytics, insights, promotion tools, booking management as paid tiers.

---

## 3. What not to sell in early stage

- Fake reviews
- Undisclosed AI product placement
- Ranking manipulation without label
- Selling private chat contents

---

## 4. Monetization readiness in schema

Already prepared conceptually:

- `Place.isSponsored`, `sponsoredUntil`
- `SponsorshipCampaign`
- `Booking`, `Payment`
- `Subscription`, entitlements

MVP may launch with **zero or light sponsorship** while measuring retention.

---

## 5. Packaging ideas (later)

| Package | Buyer | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter boost | Local café | visibility in city |
| Event push | Organizer | targeted reach |
| Pro business | Hotel/tour | dashboard + campaigns |
| Premium user | Travelers/expats | planning power / offline |

---

## 6. Competitive money angle

LocalLife does not need to “beat Google Ads” globally first.  
It can win **high-intent local moments**:

- just landed
- hungry nearby
- need transport now
- weekend planning

Those moments are monetizable if recommendations remain useful.

---

## 7. KPI for healthy monetization

- retention stays stable after ads introduction
- sponsored CTR with acceptable annoyance scores
- organic results remain majority of AI answers
- clear disclosure compliance rate = 100%
