# Phase 11 — Monetization Light — Overview

**Phase goal:** Introduce revenue without destroying trust: labeled sponsorships first.  
**Depends on:** Business profiles + stable recommendations.  
**Exit unlocks:** measured ads model before marketplace.

---

## Takes
- SponsorshipCampaign / isSponsored fields
- Business portal
- Disclosure UX in mobile + AI
- Feature flag for sponsorships

## Gives
- Business can request/buy sponsorship windows (manual ops OK at first)
- Sponsored places/events appear with clear labels
- AI mentions sponsorship explicitly when used
- Organic results remain majority
- Basic campaign analytics (impressions/clicks)
- Retention comparison before/after
