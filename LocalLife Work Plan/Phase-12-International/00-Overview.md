# Phase 12 — International — Overview

**Phase goal:** Repeat the country-pack model beyond Tunisia.  
**Depends on:** Stable multi-city ops + playbooks.  
**Exit unlocks:** global expansion capability.

---

## Takes
- Country pack architecture
- Localization (UI + content translations)
- Currency/locale/emergency numbers fields
- Local editors or partners in target country

## Gives
- Second country ACTIVE (choose strategically)
- Transport/rules/arrival packs for that country
- Multi-currency display readiness
- Language expansion as needed
- International playbook lessons captured
